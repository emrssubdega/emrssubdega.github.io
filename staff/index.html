⚖️ CCS Leave Rules Summary:
• CL Combination Rule (Rule 11): Casual Leave (CL) can ONLY be combined with Restricted Holiday (RH) or Special Casual Leave (SCL). CL cannot be combined with EL, HPL, Commuted, or Maternity leave.
• Regular Leaves (Rule 22): EL, HPL, Commuted Leave, and Maternity/Paternity/CCL may be combined together.
• Holidays in CL: Sundays & official closed holidays falling within a period of CL are not counted as leave.
(Sundays & Holidays falling in CL period are NOT counted as leave)
