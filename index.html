About Our Institution

Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS), Subdega, Sundargarh is established under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, to provide quality education to tribal students in remote areas.

Our focus is on the comprehensive development of students through high academic standards, sports, and cultural enrichment.
